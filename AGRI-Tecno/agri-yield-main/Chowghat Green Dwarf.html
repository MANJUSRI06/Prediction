🥥 Chowghat Green Dwarf (CGD) Cultivation Guide
Jump to:

🌱 Land and Soil Preparation

▼
  • Deep ploughing followed by harrowing to break clods and remove weeds.
  • Pits of 1m × 1m × 1m should be dug and filled with topsoil mixed with 10 kg FYM, 1 kg neem cake, and 500 g superphosphate.
  • Ensure proper drainage in low-lying areas to prevent waterlogging.

🧪 Soil Type and pH

▼
  • Prefers well-drained loamy or sandy loam soils.
  • Ideal pH: 5.5 to 7.5.
  • Avoid saline or highly alkaline soils; gypsum can be added to correct pH if needed.
Next section ▷

🌤 Climate Requirements

▼
  • Thrives in humid tropical climates with annual rainfall of 1000–2500 mm.
  • Optimal temperature: 27–32°C.
  • Sensitive to prolonged drought and frost; partial shade during early growth is beneficial.
Next section ▷

🌰 Seed Variety and Treatment

▼
  • Select healthy, disease-free nuts from high-yielding mother palms.
  • Soak seeds in 1% Bavistin solution for 30 minutes before sowing.
  • Germinate in nursery beds with partial shade and transplant after 9–12 months.
Next section ▷

📏 Sowing Method and Spacing

▼
  • Plant at the onset of monsoon or with assured irrigation.
  • Spacing: 6.5 m × 6.5 m (dwarf palms allow closer spacing).
  • Square or triangular planting system recommended for uniform canopy development.
Next section ▷

💊 Fertilizer Schedule

▼
  • Year 1: 50 g N, 40 g P, 120 g K per palm/month.
  • Year 2 onward: 500 g N, 320 g P, 1200 g K annually, split into 3 doses.
  • Apply micronutrients like Zn, Mg, and B if deficiency symptoms appear.
Next section ▷

💧 Irrigation Management

▼
  • Drip irrigation preferred: 40–50 liters/day/palm during dry months.
  • Mulching with coconut husk or coir pith conserves moisture.
  • Avoid over-irrigation to prevent root rot.
Next section ▷

🐛 Weed and Pest Control

▼
  • Manual weeding every 3 months or use of cover crops like cowpea.
  • Common pests: Rhinoceros beetle, red palm weevil—control with pheromone traps and neem-based sprays.
  • Diseases: Root wilt and stem bleeding—use Trichoderma and proper sanitation.
Next section ▷

⏳ Harvesting Time and Yield

▼
  • Starts bearing in 3–4 years.
  • Tender nut yield: ~60–70 nuts/palm/year.
  • Harvest every 30–45 days for consistent water quality.
Next section ▷

📦 Post-Harvest Handling

▼
  • Sort nuts by size and water content.
  • Store in cool, shaded areas to preserve freshness.
  • Transport in ventilated crates to avoid husk damage.
