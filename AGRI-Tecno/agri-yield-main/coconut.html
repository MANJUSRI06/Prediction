🥥 Coconut Varieties in India

🌱 Chowghat Green Dwarf (CGD)

Type: Traditional Dwarf Variety

Where it is grown: Kerala, Tamil Nadu (Coimbatore, Pollachi), Karnataka

About: Compact dwarf palm with early bearing (3–4 years). Produces medium-sized green-husked nuts with high water content. Suitable for home gardens and high-density plantations.

🌱 Malayan Yellow Dwarf (MYD)

Type: Traditional Dwarf Variety

Where it is grown: Tamil Nadu (Thanjavur, Villupuram), Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra

About: Fast-growing dwarf palm with bright yellow husk. Early fruiting with sweet water and soft endosperm. Ideal for small farms and urban cultivation.

🧬 Chandrasankara (COD × WCT)

Type: Hybrid (Dwarf × Tall)

Where it is grown: Tamil Nadu (Salem, Erode, Tiruppur), Kerala, Karnataka

About: Cross between COD (Chowghat Orange Dwarf) and WCT (West Coast Tall) - Combines early bearing with high copra yield.
