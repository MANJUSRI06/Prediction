🌾 Bullet Rice Cultivation Guide

1. Land and Soil Preparation

▼
  • Plough the land 2–3 times to a fine tilth.
  • Puddle with adequate moisture for proper establishment.
  • Construct strong bunds to prevent water leakage.
  • Level the land for uniform irrigation.
  • Incorporate 8–10 tons of FYM or compost; green manures may be ploughed 2–3 weeks prior.

2. Soil Type and pH

▼
  • Clay loam, sandy loam, and alluvial soils preferred.
  • pH: 5.5 – 7.5 (slightly acidic to neutral).
  • Fertile, moisture-retentive soils enriched with organic matter are ideal.

3. Climate Requirements

▼
  • Temperature: 22–35°C for tropical climate adaptation.
  • Annual rainfall: 90–120 cm or reliable irrigation.
  • Season: Samba (Aug–Jan) primarily, Kuruvai (Jun–Sep) in some areas.
  • Warm days and cooler nights during grain filling improve taste.

4. Seed Variety and Treatment

▼
  • Use pure Bullet rice seeds from trusted sources.
  • Seed rate: 20–25 kg/ha.
  • Remove floating seeds with salt-water treatment.
  • Soak seeds 10–12 hours and treat with Trichoderma viride or Pseudomonas fluorescens.
  • Pre-germinate seeds before sowing.

5. Sowing Method and Spacing

▼
  • Nursery: wet-bed or raised mat; about 600 m² nursery per hectare.
  • Transplant 20–25 day old seedlings.
  • Spacing: Normal 20×15 cm (2–3 seedlings/hill), SRI 25×25 cm (1 seedling/hill).
  • Planting depth: 2–3 cm.

6. Fertilizer Schedule

▼
  • Basal dose: FYM/compost 10 tons/ha, Neem cake 200 kg/ha.
  • Chemical: N 90–120 kg, P₂O₅ 40–50 kg, K₂O 40–60 kg per hectare.
  • Split Nitrogen: 50% at transplanting, 25% at tillering, 25% at panicle initiation.
  • Bio-fertilizers: Azospirillum and Phosphobacteria enhance growth.

7. Irrigation Management

▼
  • Keep nursery beds moist but not waterlogged.
  • Main field: maintain 2–5 cm standing water; critical stages need proper irrigation.
  • Avoid heavy stagnation at maturity to prevent lodging.
  • AWD (alternate wetting and drying) method can save water in SRI systems.

8. Weed and Pest Control

▼
  • Pre-emergence herbicide: Butachlor 1.5 L/ha within 3–5 days.
  • Manual weeding or conoweeder at 20 and 40 days.
  • Pests: Stem borer (Chlorantraniliprole/Triazophos), BPH (Imidacloprid), Leaf folder (Neem oil/Quinalphos).
  • Diseases: Blast (Tricyclazole), Sheath blight (Validamycin), Bacterial leaf blight (copper fungicides + resistant seed).

9. Harvesting Time and Yield

▼
  • Duration: 130–140 days.
  • Grains mature when straw turns yellow, panicles bend, and grains harden.
  • Manual or mechanical harvesting; thresh traditionally or with threshers.
  • Yield: 3.5–4 tons/ha under normal management, up to 5 tons/ha with SRI & improved practices.

10. Post-Harvest Handling

▼
  • Dry grains under sunlight until about 12% moisture.
  • Store in gunny bags in cool, dry godowns; use neem leaves to prevent pests.
  • Prefer semi-polished or unpolished milling to preserve nutrients.
  • Consumption: boiled rice, idli, dosa, porridge; known for good taste, higher iron & fiber.
