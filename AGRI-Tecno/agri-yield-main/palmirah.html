🌴 Palmyrah Varieties

Karuppanai palm

Type: Traditional / Local variety

Where it is grown: Tirunelveli, Thoothukudi, Ramanathapuram, Sivagangai districts of Tamil Nadu

About: Tall, hardy palmyrah palm with dark brown to black fruits. Fruits medium-sized, strong fibrous mesocarp.

Senganthal palm

Type: Traditional / Local variety

Where it is grown: Sivagangai, Pudukottai, Madurai, Thanjavur districts of Tamil Nadu

About: Medium-tall palm with reddish-brown fruits. Known for sweeter sap and larger fruit size.

Vellapanai palm

Type: Traditional / Local variety

Where it is grown: Coastal districts – Nagapattinam, Cuddalore, Thoothukudi, Ramanathapuram, Kanyakumari

About: Tall palm with light yellowish-brown fruits. Tender fruit kernel (Nungu) very soft and watery.
