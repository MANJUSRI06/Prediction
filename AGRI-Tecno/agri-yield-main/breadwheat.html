🌾 Bread Wheat Cultivation Guide

1. Land and Soil Preparation

▼

Plough the field 3–4 times to get a fine tilth.

Remove weeds and crop residues.

Use a rotavator or harrow for leveling.

Raised beds or flat sowing can be used depending on irrigation facility.

2. Soil Type and pH

▼

Grows well in loamy to clay-loam soils.

Best pH: 6.0 – 7.5.

Avoid saline/alkaline soils.

3. Climate Requirements

▼

Cool and dry climate preferred.

Temperature: 20–25°C during sowing, 14–18°C during growth, and 25–30°C for maturity.

Requires bright sunshine at grain filling stage.

Grown in rabi season (Nov–Apr).

4. Seed Variety and Treatment

▼

Popular varieties: HD 2967, PBW 343, DBW 17, WH 1105, Lok-1 (varies by state).

Seed rate: 100–120 kg/ha.

Treat seeds with Carbendazim / Thiram @ 2.5 g/kg seed for fungal protection.

Rhizobium or Azotobacter inoculation for nitrogen fixation (optional).

5. Sowing Method and Spacing

▼

Line sowing or seed drill method is best.

Row spacing: 20–22 cm.

Depth: 4–5 cm.

Sowing time: early to mid-November for best yield.

6. Fertilizer Schedule

▼

General dose (varies by soil test): NPK: 120:60:40 kg/ha.

Apply ½ Nitrogen + full P & K as basal dose.

Apply remaining ½ Nitrogen in 2 splits (at first irrigation – crown root initiation stage, and before flowering).

Use farmyard manure (FYM) 10–12 t/ha before sowing.

7. Irrigation Management

▼

Wheat is sensitive to irrigation at critical stages:

  • Crown root initiation (20–25 DAS)
  • Tillering (40–45 DAS)
  • Booting (65–70 DAS)
  • Flowering (90–95 DAS)
  • Grain filling / Dough stage (110–115 DAS)

Normally 4–5 irrigations required depending on rainfall.

8. Weed and Pest Control

▼

Weeds: Phalaris minor, Chenopodium album common.

Control by Isoproturon @ 1.0 kg/ha or Pendimethalin (pre-emergence) 1.0 L/ha.

Pests:

  • Termites → Chlorpyriphos soil treatment
  • Aphids → Dimethoate 0.03% spray

Diseases: Rusts, smut, blight → Use resistant varieties + seed treatment + fungicide spray if needed.

9. Harvesting Time and Yield

▼

Crop duration: 140–150 days.

Harvest when grains are hard, straw turns golden yellow.

Average yield: 40–55 q/ha under good management.

10. Post-harvest Handling

▼

After harvesting, dry grains under sun to reduce moisture below 12%.

Store in clean, dry, pest-free gunny bags or silos.

Treat storage structures with Malathion spray to prevent pests.
