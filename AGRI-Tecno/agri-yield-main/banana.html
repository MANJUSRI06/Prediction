🍌 Banana Varieties in India

Tip: Tap any card to open that variety’s cultivation guide.

🍌 Rasthali (Silk Banana / Malbhog)

Type: Traditional / Local Variety

Where it is grown: Tamil Nadu (Thanjavur, Trichy, Coimbatore, Salem, Erode, Tirunelveli), also Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Kerala.

About: Premium dessert banana famous for flavor and texture. Fruits are thin-skinned, creamy-white pulp.

🍌 Red Banana (Sevvazhai / Red Dacca)

Type: Traditional / Local Variety

Where it is grown: Tamil Nadu (Tirunelveli, Kanyakumari, Trichy), also Kerala, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka.

About: Tall plant with reddish pseudostem. Fruits have reddish-purple skin. Rich in Vitamin C, potassium, and beta-carotene.

🍌 Thenvalai (Honey Banana)

Type: Traditional / Local Variety

Where it is grown: Tamil Nadu (Thanjavur, Trichy, Salem, Erode), also Kerala and Karnataka.

About: Medium-tall variety, long fruits, yellow when ripe. Pulp is sweet, soft, aromatic – called "Honey Banana" for its taste.
