🌱 Country Tomato Cultivation Guide

1. Land and Soil Preparation

Plough the land 2–3 times to get a fine tilth.

Incorporate well-decomposed FYM (Farmyard Manure) 15–20 tons/acre during land preparation.

Prepare raised beds or ridges and furrows for better drainage.

2. Soil Type and pH

Grows well in red loamy and sandy loam soils.

Requires good drainage (does not tolerate waterlogging).

Optimum soil pH: 6.0 – 7.5.

3. Climate Requirements

Best suited for tropical climate.

Ideal temperature: 20°C – 30°C.

Cannot withstand frost or very heavy rainfall.

Requires 6–8 hours of sunlight daily.

4. Seed Variety and Treatment

Use traditional desi seeds of Nattu Thakkali (saved from healthy plants or bought from local seed banks).

Seed rate: 100–150 g/acre.

Seed treatment: Soak in Trichoderma viride or Pseudomonas fluorescens solution for disease protection.

For better germination, pre-soak seeds in water for 6–8 hours.

5. Sowing Method and Spacing

First, raise seedlings in a nursery bed (4–5 weeks old).

Transplant when plants are 15–20 cm tall.

Spacing:

  • Row to Row: 60 cm
  • Plant to Plant: 45 cm

Planting on ridges/furrows or raised beds is best for drainage.

6. Fertilizer Schedule

Basal Application (before transplanting):

  • FYM – 15–20 tons/acre
  • NPK (per acre): 30 kg Nitrogen, 40 kg Phosphorus, 40 kg Potassium

Top dressing:

  • 15 days after transplanting: 15 kg Nitrogen
  • 30–35 days after transplanting: 15 kg Nitrogen + 20 kg Potassium

Apply micronutrients (Boron, Zinc, Calcium) if deficiency symptoms appear.

7. Irrigation Management

Provide light irrigation immediately after transplanting.

Irrigate once every 5–7 days in summer and 10–12 days in winter.

Avoid waterlogging to prevent root rot.

Drip irrigation is highly recommended for uniform growth and water saving.

8. Weed and Pest Control

Weeds: 2–3 manual weedings or interculture operations. Mulching with straw or plastic can reduce weeds.

Common pests:

  • Fruit borer (Helicoverpa armigera) → Use neem oil spray (3%) or pheromone traps.
  • Whiteflies & aphids → Spray neem seed kernel extract (NSKE 5%).

Diseases:

  • Wilt, leaf curl, and early blight are common.
  • Use resistant seed, crop rotation, and Trichoderma application.

9. Harvesting Time and Yield

Harvest starts 60–70 days after transplanting.

Fruits are picked at the mature green or turning red stage depending on market demand.

Yield: 8–10 tons/acre (traditional varieties usually give slightly lower yields than hybrids but have better taste).

10. Post-Harvest Handling

Sort and grade fruits based on size and color.

Store in cool, dry place.

Shelf life: 5–7 days at room temperature.

Can be used for making sambar, rasam, chutney, pickle, and processing (sauces, pulp).
