🌾 Sugarcane Varieties in India

CO 18009

Type: Hybrid / Commercial Variety

Where it is grown: Widely cultivated in Tamil Nadu and adopted in Karnataka & Andhra Pradesh.

About: High-yielding sugarcane hybrid released by TNAU. Mid-late maturing with robust cane growth and thick stalks.

CO 11015 (Atulya)

Type: Hybrid / Open-pollinated

Where it is grown: Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Kerala, Karnataka, Gujarat, Maharashtra, MP.

About: Early-maturing, high-yielding clone developed by ICAR–SBI, Coimbatore. Tall canes with wax-coated stalks, suited to drought-prone areas.

Filipino Black Sugarcane

Type: Traditional / Exotic Commercial Variety

Where it is grown: Origin: Philippines; also in Bangladesh and small-scale gardens in India.

About: Distinctive dark brown to black culms. Tall (12–20 ft), soft stalks with high juice content. Popular for chewing & juice production.
