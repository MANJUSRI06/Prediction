🌱 Hill Garlic Cultivation Guide

1. Land and Soil Preparation

  • Plough the land 3–4 times to get a fine tilth.
  • Add well-decomposed farmyard manure (FYM) @ 15–20 t/ha during final ploughing.
  • Form raised beds or ridges for better drainage in hill regions.

2. Soil Type and pH

  • Best suited for well-drained sandy loam or clay loam soils.
  • Ideal soil pH: 6.0–7.5.

3. Climate Requirements

  • Grows well in cool climates (15–25°C).
  • Hill garlic requires short-day conditions for proper bulb development.
  • Suitable for high altitude regions (1200–2000 m above MSL).

4. Seed Variety and Treatment

  • Variety: Hill Garlic (GI-tagged from Perambalur, TN).
  • Use healthy, disease-free cloves for sowing.
  • Treat cloves with Trichoderma viride (4 g/kg) or fungicide like Carbendazim (2 g/kg) to prevent soil-borne diseases.

5. Sowing Method and Spacing

  • Planting Season: October–November in hills.
  • Plant cloves at 2–3 cm depth with the pointed side up.
  • Spacing: 15 cm × 10 cm (row × plant).
  • Seed rate: 500–600 kg cloves per hectare.

6. Fertilizer Schedule

  • Apply FYM @ 20 t/ha before sowing.
  • Recommended dose: 100:50:50 NPK kg/ha.
  • Apply 50% N + full P & K as basal.
  • Remaining 50% N in two splits (30 & 60 days after planting).
  • Foliar spray of micronutrients (Zn, B) improves bulb quality.

7. Irrigation Management

  • Provide light irrigation immediately after sowing.
  • Subsequent irrigations every 7–10 days depending on soil moisture.
  • Avoid waterlogging, as garlic is highly sensitive to excess water.

8. Weed and Pest Control

  • Perform 2–3 manual weedings during the crop cycle.
  • Use mulching (paddy straw/black polythene) to suppress weeds.
  • Common pests: Thrips, onion maggot → Control with Neem oil spray (3%) or recommended insecticides.
  • Diseases: White rot, downy mildew → Manage with Carbendazim or Mancozeb sprays.

9. Harvesting Time and Yield

  • Crop duration: 120–150 days.
  • Harvest when 50–70% tops dry and fall.
  • Expected yield: 5–6 t/ha (dry bulbs).

10. Post-harvest Handling

  • After harvest, bulbs are cured under shade for 10–15 days to remove excess moisture.
  • Clean and trim roots & shoots.
  • Store in well-ventilated, dry conditions.
  • Properly cured bulbs can be stored for 6–8 months.
