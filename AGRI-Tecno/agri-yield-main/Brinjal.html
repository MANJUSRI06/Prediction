🍆 Brinjal Varieties in India

Tip: Tap any card to open that variety’s cultivation guide.

🌱 Indian Eggplant

Type: Open Pollinated / Traditional (many local landraces)

Where it is grown: Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Maharashtra, West Bengal, Bihar, Uttar Pradesh.

About: Usually round to oval fruits, deep purple, green, or striped. Plants are bushy, medium height, dense branching.

🌱 American Eggplant (Globe)

Type: Hybrid / Commercial

Where it is grown: USA, Europe, and in India (Karnataka, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu – peri-urban farms).

About: Large, oval, glossy dark purple fruits. Plants are vigorous, semi-upright, often require staking.

🌱 Chinese Eggplant

Type: Hybrid / Open Pollinated (long slender types)

Where it is grown: China, Southeast Asia, and India (Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Kerala – export farms).

About: Long, slender, cylindrical fruits, light purple or violet. Semi-erect plants, continuous yield.
