🌿 Sugarcane (CO 11015) Cultivation Guide
🌐

1. Land and Soil Preparation

Select deep, fertile, and well-drained soils for better yield.

Plough the land 3–4 times to achieve fine tilth and remove perennial weeds.

Level the land properly and form ridges and furrows at 90–120 cm spacing.

Apply 25–30 tonnes FYM/ha during final ploughing to enrich soil fertility.

2. Soil Type and pH

Best suited soils: Loamy, clay loam, or alluvial soils rich in organic matter.

Ideal pH: 6.5 – 7.5.

Avoid saline, alkaline, or waterlogged soils.

3. Climate Requirements

Thrives in tropical and subtropical climates.

Optimum temperature: 20°C – 32°C.

Rainfall requirement: 1000–1500 mm annually or assured irrigation.

Sensitive to prolonged drought → irrigation is essential for optimum yield.

4. Seed Variety and Treatment

Variety: CO 11015 (released by Sugarcane Breeding Institute, Coimbatore).

Features: High cane yield, good ratoon performance, and better juice quality with high sugar recovery.

Propagation: By setts (2–3 bud cuttings).

Treatment:

  • Soak setts in 0.25% Carbendazim (Bavistin) or Trichoderma solution before planting to avoid sett rot.
  • Hot water treatment at 50°C for 30 minutes also helps in disease control.

5. Sowing Method and Spacing

Planting season:

  • January–February (Spring planting).
  • July–August (Autumn planting).

Spacing:

  • 90 cm between ridges for normal planting.
  • 120 cm for wide-row planting with intercrops.

Depth: Place setts at 10–15 cm depth in moist furrows, cover with soil, and irrigate immediately.

6. Fertilizer Schedule

Basal application: 25–30 tonnes FYM/ha before planting.

Chemical fertilizers (per hectare):

  • N: 280 kg
  • P₂O₅: 100 kg
  • K₂O: 120 kg

Split application:

  • 25% N + 100% P + 50% K at planting.
  • Remaining N and K in 3–4 splits at 45, 90, and 120 days after planting.

Apply micronutrients (Zn, Fe, B) in deficient soils for improved cane quality.

7. Irrigation Management

Irrigation interval: 7–10 days in summer, 12–15 days in winter.

Critical stages: Germination, tillering, grand growth, and ripening.

Drip irrigation + fertigation is recommended for better water and nutrient efficiency.

Avoid water stagnation → reduces risk of root and sett rot.

8. Weed and Pest Control

Weed management:

  • Apply Atrazine (1.0 kg/ha) or Metribuzin (1.0 kg/ha) as pre-emergence herbicide.
  • Do intercultural operations and earthing-up at 90 and 150 days.

Pests: Early shoot borer, top borer, internode borer, white grubs.

Control: Release Trichogramma chilonis, apply neem formulations or recommended insecticides.

Diseases: Red rot, smut, wilt, and sett rot.

Control: Disease-free setts, crop rotation, sett treatment, and rogueing of diseased clumps.

9. Harvesting Time and Yield

Crop duration: 10–11 months (early maturing).

Harvest when:

  • Canes are hard and mature.
  • Leaves turn yellowish and lower leaves dry.

Average yield: 120–140 tonnes/ha.

Sugar recovery: 11–12% (high recovery makes it suitable for sugar industries).

10. Post-harvest Handling

Harvest canes at ground level to include sugar-rich basal portion.

Remove dry leaves and tops; tie canes into bundles for transport.

Crush canes within 24–36 hours of harvest for maximum sugar recovery.

Trash and tops can be used for mulching, compost, or cattle feed.
