🌽 Sweet Corn Cultivation Guide

1. Land and Soil Preparation

Sweet corn requires a fine, fertile seedbed to ensure good germination.

Plough 2–3 times to achieve fine tilth and remove weeds.

Apply 10–15 tonnes/ha of FYM or compost at the final ploughing.

Raised beds or ridges are recommended in heavy rainfall zones to prevent waterlogging.

2. Soil Type and pH

Prefers fertile sandy loam to loamy soils rich in organic matter.

Poor growth in saline and alkaline soils.

Optimum pH: 5.5–7.0.

3. Climate Requirements

Sweet corn is a short-duration, warm-season crop.

Optimum temperature: 18–30°C.

Very sensitive to drought and high temperatures during pollination.

Requires cool nights and warm days for good sweetness.

Can be grown in both Kharif and Rabi seasons under irrigation.

4. Seed Variety and Treatment

Popular Varieties: Madhuri, Sugar-75, Priya, WinOrange, TNAU hybrids.

Seed rate: 8–10 kg/ha.

Seed treatment:

  • Fungicide: Thiram or Carbendazim @ 2–3 g/kg seed.
  • Biofertilizer: Azospirillum/Azotobacter inoculation.
  • In pest-prone soils: Imidacloprid @ 5 ml/kg seed.

5. Sowing Method and Spacing

Direct sowing by dibbling or seed drill.

Spacing: 60 × 20 cm or 60 × 30 cm depending on soil fertility.

Sowing depth: 3–4 cm.

Plant population: 55,000–60,000 plants/ha for good cob size.

6. Fertilizer Schedule

Sweet corn is a nutrient-demanding crop due to its quick growth.

Basal dose: FYM 10–15 t/ha + NPK (60:60:40 kg/ha).

Top dressing: Apply remaining nitrogen in 3 splits:

  • At 20 DAS (early growth)
  • At tasseling stage
  • At cob initiation stage

Total requirement: 120:60:40 kg/ha (NPK). Foliar sprays of micronutrients (Zn, B) improve sweetness and cob filling.

7. Irrigation Management

Requires frequent irrigation as roots are shallow.

Critical irrigation stages:

  • Germination
  • Knee-high stage
  • Tasseling
  • Silking
  • Cob filling

In summer: Irrigate every 5–7 days; in winter: 10–12 days interval. Drip irrigation is ideal for maintaining soil moisture and fertigation.

8. Weed and Pest Control

Weeds: Perform 2 hand weedings at 20 & 40 DAS. Pre-emergence Atrazine 1 kg a.i./ha controls early weeds.

Pests: Fall armyworm (serious in sweet corn) – control with Emamectin benzoate or Spinosad sprays. Stem borer – Carbofuran granules in whorls.

Diseases: Turcicum leaf blight, rust, and downy mildew are common. Spray Mancozeb or Propiconazole when symptoms appear.

9. Harvesting Time and Yield

Duration: 70–90 days (shorter than field maize).

Harvest at milk stage (20–24 days after silking) when grains are soft, tender, and sugary.

Signs: Kernels exude milky juice when pressed, husks green, silk brown.

Yield: Green cobs: 8–12 t/ha; Grain yield (if dried): 20–30 q/ha.

10. Post-Harvest Handling

Harvest cobs early morning for best taste. Sweetness deteriorates quickly after harvest due to sugar-starch conversion.

Store at 0–4°C for retaining sweetness.

Marketed fresh, frozen, canned, or processed into corn soup, baby corn, and snacks.
