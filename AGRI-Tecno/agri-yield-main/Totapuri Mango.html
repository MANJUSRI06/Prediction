🌱 Totapuri Mango Cultivation Guide

1. Land and Soil Preparation

  • Deep ploughing (1–2 times) to loosen soil.
  • Remove weeds and stumps.
  • Pits of 1 × 1 × 1 m prepared at 8 × 8 m spacing.
  • Fill pits with topsoil + FYM (25–30 kg) + neem cake (1–2 kg) + single super phosphate (1 kg).

2. Soil Type and pH

  • Well-drained sandy loam to clay loam soils best.
  • Ideal pH: 6.5–7.5.
  • Avoid waterlogged and saline soils.

3. Climate Requirements

  • Tropical to subtropical climate.
  • Optimum temperature: 24–30°C.
  • Rainfall: 750–2500 mm annually.
  • Dry spell before flowering is beneficial.

4. Seed Variety and Treatment

  • Variety: Totapuri Mango (Ginimoothi, Bangalora).
  • Grafted plants from certified nurseries recommended.
  • Treat grafted seedlings with fungicide (Carbendazim 1 g/L) before planting to prevent root rot.

5. Planting Method and Spacing

  • Season: June–July (monsoon) or December–January (irrigated areas).
  • Spacing: 8 × 8 m (156 plants/ha).
  • Plant grafts at same depth as in nursery bag.

6. Fertilizer Schedule

  • FYM: 25–30 kg/plant/year.
  • NPK dose (per tree, after 3rd year):
  • 1st year: 100 g N, 50 g P, 100 g K.
  • Increase gradually up to 1 kg N, 500 g P, 1 kg K for adult trees.
  • Apply fertilizers in 2 splits: before flowering & after fruit set.
  • Micronutrients: Foliar spray of ZnSO₄ (0.5%) and Borax (0.2%) for better fruit quality.

7. Irrigation Management

  • Young plants: water every 7–10 days in dry season.
  • Bearing trees: irrigate at critical stages – flowering, fruit set, fruit development.
  • Avoid irrigation 15 days before flowering to encourage profuse bloom.
  • Drip irrigation recommended for water efficiency.

8. Weed and Pest Control

  • Mulching with dry leaves or black polythene to suppress weeds.
  • Pests: Mango hopper, fruit fly, mealy bug → control with neem oil 5% or recommended insecticides.
  • Diseases: Powdery mildew, anthracnose → spray with Mancozeb or Carbendazim.

9. Harvesting and Yield

  • Bearing age: 4–5 years after planting.
  • Fruit maturity: 90–100 days after fruit set.
  • Average yield: 8–10 t/ha in early years; 12–15 t/ha in full bearing orchards.
  • Totapuri fruits are medium to large (250–400 g), parrot-beak shaped.

10. Post-harvest Handling

  • Harvest at mature green or yellow-green stage.
  • Grade fruits based on size and shape.
  • Pack in ventilated wooden/plastic crates.
  • Shelf life: 7–10 days at room temperature; 2–3 weeks under cold storage (12–13°C, 85–90% RH).
  • Processed into pulp, juice, pickle, squash, and jams for domestic and export markets.
