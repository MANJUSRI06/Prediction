🌾 Ponni Rice Cultivation Guide

1. Land and Soil Preparation

  • Plough the field 2–3 times for fine tilth.
  • Flood and puddle the soil to suppress weeds and reduce water loss.
  • Level the land for uniform irrigation and nutrient distribution.
  • Construct strong bunds to retain irrigation water.
  • Apply 8–10 tons/ha FYM, compost, or green manure 2–3 weeks before transplanting.

2. Soil Type and pH

  • Clay loam, alluvial, and sandy loam with good fertility and water retention.
  • pH: 5.5 – 7.5 (slightly acidic to neutral).
  • Moderately sensitive to salinity; low-salt soils are preferred.

3. Climate Requirements

  • Temperature: 20–35°C.
  • Annual rainfall: 100–120 cm or good irrigation.
  • Seasons: Kuruvai (Jun–Sep) and Samba (Aug–Jan).
  • Warm days and cooler nights improve grain filling and cooking quality.

4. Seed Variety and Treatment

  • Popular varieties: ADTP 36, ADT 37, ADT 43, IR 20, CO 50, CR 1009, White Ponni.
  • Seed rate: 20–25 kg/ha for nursery.
  • Float seeds in salt water to remove unfilled grains.
  • Soak seeds 12 hours, incubate 24 hours, and treat with Trichoderma viride or Carbendazim.
  • Use Pseudomonas fluorescens for bacterial resistance.

5. Sowing Method and Spacing

  • Nursery: Raised mat or wet-bed nursery (600–700 m² per hectare).
  • Transplant 20–25 day old seedlings.
  • Spacing: Normal 20×15 cm (2–3 seedlings/hill), SRI 25×25 cm (1 seedling/hill).
  • Planting depth: 2–3 cm.

6. Fertilizer Schedule

  • Organic: FYM/compost 10 tons/ha, Neem cake 200 kg/ha.
  • Chemical: N 120–150 kg, P₂O₅ 40–50 kg, K₂O 40–60 kg per hectare.
  • Split N application: 50% at transplanting, 25% at tillering, 25% at panicle initiation.
  • Bio-fertilizers: Azospirillum and Phosphobacteria (2 kg/ha each with FYM).

7. Irrigation Management

  • Nursery: Keep moist with shallow irrigation.
  • Main field: 2–5 cm standing water, 5–7 cm during panicle initiation and flowering.
  • Use AWD for water saving.
  • Stop irrigation 7–10 days before harvest.

8. Weed and Pest Control

  • Pre-emergence herbicide: Butachlor 1.5 L/ha within 3–5 days.
  • Manual or mechanical weeding at 20 and 40 days.
  • Pests: Stem borer (Chlorantraniliprole/Triazophos), BPH (Imidacloprid/Neem), Leaf folder (Neem oil/Monocrotophos).
  • Diseases: Blast (Tricyclazole), Sheath blight (Validamycin), Bacterial leaf blight (Streptocycline + bio-treatment).

9. Harvesting Time and Yield

  • Duration: 120–135 days depending on variety.
  • Maturity: Grains turn golden yellow, panicles bend, straw dries.
  • Harvest manually or mechanically; threshing manually or mechanically.
  • Yield: Conventional 4.5–5 tons/ha, SRI 6–7 tons/ha.

10. Post-Harvest Handling

  • Sun-dry grains to 12–14% moisture.
  • Store in ventilated, clean gunny bags. Neem leaves or fumigation for pest control.
  • Processing: Semi-polished or fully polished depending on market.
  • Usage: Table rice, sambar rice, curd rice, lemon rice, tamarind rice; high export value.
