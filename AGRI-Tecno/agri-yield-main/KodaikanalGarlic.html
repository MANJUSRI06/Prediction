🌱 Kodaikanal Garlic Cultivation Guide

1. Land and Soil Preparation

  • Plough the land 3–4 times to achieve a fine tilth.
  • Incorporate well-decomposed FYM @ 15–20 t/ha during the last ploughing.
  • Raised beds/ridges are essential for proper drainage in hilly terrain.

2. Soil Type and pH

  • Prefers sandy loam to loamy soils, well-drained and fertile.
  • Optimum soil pH: 6.0–7.5.

3. Climate Requirements

  • Grows best in cool hill climates (12–20°C).
  • Requires short-day conditions for bulb formation.
  • Suitable for altitudes of 1200–2000 m (Kodaikanal hills).

4. Seed Variety and Treatment

  • Variety: Kodaikanal Garlic (GI-tagged from Dindigul district, TN).
  • Bulbs are small-sized with intense pungency and long storage life.
  • Select healthy cloves (free from fungal infection).
  • Treat with Trichoderma viride (4 g/kg) or Carbendazim (2 g/kg) before sowing.

5. Sowing Method and Spacing

  • Sowing season: October–November.
  • Plant cloves at a depth of 2–3 cm, pointed side upward.
  • Spacing: 15 cm × 10 cm (row × plant).
  • Seed rate: 500–600 kg cloves/ha.

6. Fertilizer Schedule

  • Apply FYM @ 20 t/ha at field preparation.
  • Fertilizer recommendation: 100:50:50 NPK kg/ha.
  • 50% Nitrogen + full Phosphorus & Potash as basal.
  • Remaining Nitrogen in 2 equal splits (30 & 60 days after planting).
  • Foliar spray of Zn & Boron improves bulb size and storage quality.

7. Irrigation Management

  • Light irrigation immediately after planting.
  • Subsequent irrigations at 7–10 day intervals depending on soil moisture.
  • Avoid water stagnation (leads to bulb rot).

8. Weed and Pest Control

  • Manual weeding (2–3 times) or mulching with paddy straw/black polythene to reduce weeds.
  • Pests: Thrips, onion maggot – managed with Neem oil spray (3%) or recommended insecticides.
  • Diseases: White rot, purple blotch – managed by Mancozeb or Copper oxychloride sprays.

9. Harvesting Time and Yield

  • Crop duration: 120–150 days.
  • Harvest when 50–70% of tops dry and lodge.
  • Average yield: 5–7 t/ha of dry bulbs.

10. Post-harvest Handling

  • After harvest, bulbs should be cured under shade for 10–15 days to dry outer skins.
  • Trim roots and shoots, then bundle or net-pack for storage.
  • Store in well-ventilated, dry conditions.
  • Kodaikanal garlic has excellent storage life (up to 8 months) due to its compact bulbs.
