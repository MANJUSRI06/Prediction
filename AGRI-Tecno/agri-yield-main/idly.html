🍚 Idly Rice Cultivation Guide

1. Land and Soil Preparation

  • Start with 2–3 deep ploughings to break soil clods.
  • Puddle the field by flooding and tilling to create a soft muddy bed.
  • Level the land for uniform water depth and proper crop growth.
  • Construct strong bunds and channels for irrigation.
  • Apply 8–10 tons of FYM or compost 2–3 weeks before transplanting.

2. Soil Type and pH

  • Preferred soil: Fertile clay loam, alluvial soil, or sandy loam.
  • pH: Slightly acidic to neutral (5.5 – 7.5).
  • Soils rich in organic matter produce starchy rice suitable for idli batter.

3. Climate Requirements

  • Optimal temperature: 20–35°C.
  • Annual rainfall: 100–120 cm or assured irrigation.
  • Season: Kuruvai (Jun–Sep) and Samba (Aug–Jan) in Tamil Nadu.
  • High humidity during vegetative and reproductive stages ensures good yield.

4. Seed Variety and Treatment

  • Popular varieties: ADT 36, ADT 37, ADT 43, ADT 45, CO 50, CR 1009 Sub-1, White Ponni, IR 20.
  • Seed rate: 20–25 kg/ha for nursery sowing.
  • Salt-water treatment to remove unfilled grains.
  • Fungicide treatment: Carbendazim (2 g/kg) or Bavistin.
  • Bio-treatment with Trichoderma viride or Pseudomonas fluorescens improves resistance.
  • Pre-germinate seeds by soaking 12 hours and incubate 24 hours before sowing.

5. Sowing Method and Spacing

  • Use raised nursery beds or wet-bed nurseries; 600–700 m² per hectare.
  • Transplant 20–25 days old seedlings.
  • Spacing: Normal: 20×15 cm (2–3 seedlings/hill), SRI: 25×25 cm (1 seedling/hill).
  • Planting depth: 2–3 cm for proper establishment.

6. Fertilizer Schedule

  • Basal: FYM 10 tons/ha, Neem cake 200–250 kg/ha.
  • Chemical fertilizers per hectare: N 120–150 kg, P₂O₅ 40–50 kg, K₂O 40–60 kg.
  • Split application: 50% N + full P + 50% K at transplanting, 25% N + 25% K at tillering, remaining 25% N at panicle initiation.
  • Bio-fertilizers: Azospirillum and Phosphobacteria at 2 kg/ha each mixed with FYM.

7. Irrigation Management

  • Keep nursery beds moist but not waterlogged.
  • Main field: Maintain 2–5 cm standing water; 5–7 cm during panicle initiation and flowering.
  • Use alternate wetting and drying (AWD) to save water and improve soil aeration.
  • Stop irrigation 7–10 days before harvest for proper grain maturation.

8. Weed and Pest Control

  • Pre-emergence herbicide: Butachlor @1.5 L/ha within 3–5 days after transplanting.
  • Hand weeding at 20 and 40 days.
  • Pests: Stem borer (Carbofuran or Chlorantraniliprole), Leaf folder (Neem oil or Monocrotophos), BPH (Imidacloprid/Thiamethoxam).
  • Diseases: Blast (Tricyclazole 0.6 g/l), Sheath blight (Validamycin), Bacterial leaf blight (seed treatment + Streptocycline).

9. Harvesting Time and Yield

  • Duration: 120–135 days depending on variety.
  • Harvest when grains turn golden-yellow, panicles bend slightly, and straw dries.
  • Use sickles/harvesters, thresh, and dry grains to 12–14% moisture.
  • Yield: Normal: 4.5–5 tons/ha, SRI: 6–7 tons/ha with proper management.

10. Post-Harvest Handling

  • Sun-dry grains to 12% moisture.
  • Store in gunny bags in cool, ventilated godowns with neem leaves for pest protection.
  • Mildly polish to retain nutrition and starch for idlis/dosas.
  • Usage: Idli, dosa, paniyaram, and other South Indian dishes.
