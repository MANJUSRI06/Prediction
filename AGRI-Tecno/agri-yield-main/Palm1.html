🌴 Palmyrah Cultivation Guide

1. Land and Soil Preparation

One-time deep ploughing + harrowing.

Pits (1×1×1 m) filled with topsoil + FYM.

2. Soil Type and pH

Sandy loam / red soil.

pH: 6.5 – 8.5.

3. Climate Requirements

Semi-arid and dry zones.

Temperature: 35–45 °C.

Rainfall: 600–1000 mm.

4. Seed Variety and Treatment

Black/dark brown seeds.

Soak 2–3 days in water before sowing.

5. Sowing Method and Spacing

Direct sowing or nursery planting.

Spacing: 9 m × 9 m.

6. Fertilizer Schedule

FYM 10–15 kg/palm/year.

NPK @ 100:50:50 g/palm/year after 5 years.

7. Irrigation Management

Survives drought.

Irrigate once in 12–15 days during summer for better sap flow.

8. Weed and Pest Control

Regular ring weeding.

White grub, palm weevil → neem cake & prophylactic insecticide.

9. Harvesting Time and Yield

Bearing starts: 12–15 years.

Sap yield: 3–5 liters/day/palm.

Fruit yield: 250–300 fruits/palm/year.

10. Post-harvest Handling

Sap → toddy, jaggery.

Leaves → mats, baskets.

Fiber → ropes, brushes.
