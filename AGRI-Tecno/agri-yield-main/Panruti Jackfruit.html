🍈 Panruti Jackfruit Cultivation Guide

1. Land and Soil Preparation

Select fertile land with good drainage.

Pits of 3 ft × 3 ft × 3 ft are dug and filled with topsoil, FYM (10–15 kg), and Neem cake (1–2 kg) before planting.

2. Soil Type and pH

Thrives in red loamy, sandy loam, and lateritic soils.

Soil pH: 6.0 – 7.0 (slightly acidic).

3. Climate Requirements

Grows well in tropical humid climate with high sunshine.

Temperature: 22°C – 36°C.

Rainfall requirement: 1000–2000 mm annually.

Special Feature: Panruti’s climate supports year-round fruiting, making it unique.

4. Seed Variety and Treatment

Propagation through grafting or budding to maintain Panruti’s special traits.

Seeds are used for rootstock; scions from elite Panruti varieties are grafted.

Treat planting material with Trichoderma / fungicide before planting.

5. Sowing Method and Spacing

Spacing: 8 m × 8 m (approx. 150 trees/ha).

Grafted plants are preferred over seedlings to ensure uniform fruit quality.

6. Fertilizer Schedule

Apply FYM (20–25 kg/tree/year) at monsoon onset.

NPK dose (per year per tree):

  • Nitrogen: 200–250 g
  • Phosphorus: 150 g
  • Potassium: 200–250 g

Split application during June–July and December–January.

7. Irrigation Management

Irrigation needed once in 10–12 days during summer.

Avoid stagnant water around tree base.

Mulching with organic matter helps conserve moisture.

8. Weed and Pest Control

Remove weeds manually around tree base.

Pests: fruit borer, shoot borer, mealy bugs – controlled with neem oil spray or organic pesticides.

Diseases: fruit rot, stem rot can be prevented by applying 1% Bordeaux mixture.

9. Harvesting Time and Yield

Fruiting starts from 4th–5th year in grafted plants.

Panruti jackfruit is special as trees bear fruits multiple times a year (not just seasonal).

Average yield: 60–80 fruits per tree annually.

Fruit size: 10–30 kg each, with sweet golden-yellow bulbs.

Yield potential: 25–30 tonnes/ha.

10. Post-harvest Handling

Harvest when fruit develops a strong aroma and gives a dull sound on tapping.

Handle gently to prevent skin injury.

Fruits can be stored for 5–7 days at room temperature, and up to 2–3 weeks under cold storage.

Used for table purpose, chips, sweets, processed pulp, jams, and export due to its high sweetness and good keeping quality.
