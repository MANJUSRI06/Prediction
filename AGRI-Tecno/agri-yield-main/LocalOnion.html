🧅 Shallot Cultivation in India – Detailed Guide

🌱 Land and Soil Preparation

  • Land must be well-drained (shallots very sensitive to waterlogging).
  • Plough 3–4 times → fine tilth prepare pannanum.
  • Form raised beds or ridges & furrows for good drainage.
  • Add FYM/compost (20–25 t/ha) before last plough.
  • Incorporate neem cake (250 kg/ha) to control soil-borne pests.

🧪 Soil Type and pH

  • Best soils: sandy loam and loamy soils with good organic matter.
  • pH: 6.0 – 7.0.
  • Avoid clayey or heavy soils → bulb splitting & rotting chances.
  • Loose soil helps in bulb multiplication.

🌤 Climate Requirements

  • Shallots prefer tropical & subtropical climate.
  • Moderate temperature (20–30°C) is best.
  • Very high temperature → bolting and smaller bulbs.
  • Heavy rainfall / high humidity → fungal diseases increase.
  • Dry climate during bulb maturity ensures long storage.

🌰 Seed Material and Treatment

  • Use healthy, disease-free bulbs (6–10 g each) as planting material.
  • Seed rate: 8–10 quintals of bulbs/ha.
  • Bulb treatment: Dip in fungicide (Carbendazim 2g/litre) or Trichoderma solution for biocontrol.
  • Improved varieties: Co-1, Co-2, Co-3, Periyakulam-1, Arka Bindu, Agrifound Dark Red.

📏 Planting Method and Spacing

  • Planting season: Sept–Oct (kharif), Jan–Feb (rabi) in South India.
  • Plant bulbs directly in the main field (no nursery).
  • Spacing: Row-to-row: 20 cm, Plant-to-plant: 10 cm.
  • Depth: 2–3 cm, neck upward.
  • Method: Ridge & furrow or flatbed system.

💊 Fertilizer Schedule

  • Organic manure: FYM/compost @ 20–25 t/ha.
  • Inorganic fertilizer (per hectare): N: 100–120 kg, P₂O₅: 50–60 kg, K₂O: 50–60 kg.
  • Application: Full P & K + half N at planting; Remaining N in 2 splits (30 & 45 days).
  • Foliar spray of micronutrients (Zn, B) improves bulb multiplication & storage.

💧 Irrigation Management

  • First irrigation immediately after planting.
  • Frequency: 7–8 days in cool season, 5–6 days in summer.
  • Critical stages: bulb initiation & development.
  • Stop irrigation 10 days before harvest to improve curing.
  • Drip irrigation + mulching saves water & controls weeds.

🐛 Weed and Pest Control

  • Weeds: 2 hand weedings at 20 & 40 days after planting. Mulching reduces weed growth.
  • Herbicide: Pendimethalin (1 kg a.i./ha) as pre-emergence.
  • Pests: Thrips, Onion maggot, Aphids – neem oil, Imidacloprid, Chlorpyriphos.
  • Diseases: Purple blotch, Stemphylium blight, Leaf spot – rotate Mancozeb & Metalaxyl. Avoid excess irrigation for neck rot.

⏳ Harvesting Time and Yield

  • Crop duration: 70–90 days after planting.
  • Harvest signs: Tops dry, yellow & fall; bulbs mature with dry outer scales.
  • Uproot bulbs carefully to avoid damage.
  • Yield: 10–15 tonnes/ha.

📦 Post-Harvest Handling

  • Curing: Dry bulbs in shade or sun for 5–7 days → neck shrivels & outer scales dry.
  • Cleaning: Remove roots & dry leaves, grade bulbs by size.
  • Storage: Well-ventilated bamboo baskets / mud structures. Keep bulbs dry to prevent sprouting.
  • Modern storage: ventilated houses with bottom airflow give 3–4 months shelf life.
