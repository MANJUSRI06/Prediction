🌴 Palmyrah Cultivation Guide

1. Land and Soil Preparation

Plough + level well.

Pits (1×1×1 m) enriched with FYM.

2. Soil Type and pH

Red sandy soil.

pH: 6.0–7.5.

3. Climate Requirements

Semi-arid with moderate rainfall.

Temperature: 30–40 °C.

4. Seed Variety and Treatment

Reddish-brown large seeds.

Hot water treatment (30 min) improves germination.

5. Sowing Method and Spacing

Pit method preferred.

Spacing: 8 m × 8 m.

6. Fertilizer Schedule

FYM 20 kg/palm/year.

Rock phosphate + neem cake.

7. Irrigation Management

Water once in 10–12 days during tapping season.

Mulching conserves soil moisture.

8. Weed and Pest Control

Intercropping with pulses suppresses weeds.

Leaf beetles → neem oil spray.

9. Harvesting Time and Yield

Fruiting: May–June.

Sap yield: 4–6 liters/day/palm (sweeter sap).

Fruit yield: 300–350 fruits/palm/year.

10. Post-harvest Handling

Sap → palm sugar, jaggery.

Leaves → roofing sheets, mats.

Petiole → handicrafts.
