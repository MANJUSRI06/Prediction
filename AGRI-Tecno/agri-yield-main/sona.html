🌾 Sona Masoori Rice Cultivation Guide

1. Land and Soil Preparation

  • Plough the land 2–3 times to achieve fine tilth.
  • Construct strong bunds and irrigation channels for efficient water control.
  • Level the field to prevent water stagnation and ensure uniform growth.
  • Apply 8–10 tons/ha of FYM or compost. Green manuring improves nitrogen content.

2. Soil Type and pH

  • Preferred soils: Fertile alluvial, clay loam, or sandy loam.
  • pH range: 5.5 – 7.5 (slightly acidic to neutral).
  • Soils rich in organic matter enhance aroma and cooking quality.

3. Climate Requirements

  • Temperature: 20–35°C in warm tropical climate.
  • Rainfall: 80–120 cm annually or assured irrigation.
  • Seasons: Kharif (Jun–Nov) and Rabi (Dec–Apr).
  • Thrives with warm days and slightly cooler nights to improve grain aroma.

4. Seed Variety and Treatment

  • Popular varieties: BPT 5204, Sona, HMT Sona Masoori, Sona Mahsuri.
  • Seed rate: 20–25 kg/ha for nursery sowing.
  • Remove unfilled grains using salt-water floatation.
  • Soak seeds for 12 hours, incubate 24 hours, and treat with Trichoderma or Carbendazim.
  • Pseudomonas fluorescens treatment improves bacterial resistance.

5. Sowing Method and Spacing

  • Use wet-bed or mat nursery (600–700 m² per hectare).
  • Transplant 20–25 day old seedlings.
  • Spacing: Normal 20×15 cm (2–3 seedlings/hill), SRI 25×25 cm (1 seedling/hill).
  • Planting depth: 2–3 cm.

6. Fertilizer Schedule

  • Organic: FYM/compost 8–10 tons/ha + Neem cake 200 kg/ha.
  • Chemical: N 120–150 kg, P₂O₅ 40–50 kg, K₂O 40–60 kg per hectare.
  • Split Nitrogen: 50% at transplanting, 25% at tillering, 25% at panicle initiation.
  • Bio-fertilizers: Azospirillum & Phosphobacteria 2 kg/ha mixed with FYM.

7. Irrigation Management

  • Keep nursery beds moist but not waterlogged.
  • Main field: 2–5 cm standing water; proper irrigation during tillering, panicle initiation, and flowering.
  • Avoid water stagnation at maturity; stop irrigation 7–10 days before harvest.
  • AWD method can save water and improve soil aeration.

8. Weed and Pest Control

  • Pre-emergence herbicide: Butachlor 1.5 L/ha within 3–5 days.
  • Manual weeding or conoweeder at 20 and 40 days.
  • Pests: Stem borer (Chlorantraniliprole/Carbofuran), BPH (Imidacloprid/Neem), Leaf folder (Neem oil/Quinalphos).
  • Diseases: Blast (Tricyclazole), Sheath blight (Validamycin), Bacterial leaf blight (copper sprays + resistant seeds).

9. Harvesting Time and Yield

  • Duration: 120–135 days depending on variety.
  • Maturity: Golden yellow grains, bending panicles, dried straw.
  • Harvest manually or mechanically; threshing done manually or using machines.
  • Yield: Normal 4.5–5 tons/ha, SRI 6–7 tons/ha.

10. Post-Harvest Handling

  • Dry grains to 12–14% moisture in sunlight.
  • Store in clean, dry gunny bags; use neem leaves or fumigation to protect from pests.
  • Processing: Semi-polished or polished grains for market.
  • Consumption: Daily rice, pulao, fried rice, South Indian dishes; light and non-sticky grains suitable for export.
