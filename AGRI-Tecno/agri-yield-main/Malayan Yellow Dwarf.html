🥥 Malayan Yellow Dwarf (MYD) Cultivation Guide

🌱 Land and Soil Preparation

  • Deep tillage followed by leveling and pit preparation.
  • Pits: 1m³ filled with topsoil, FYM, neem cake, and bone meal.
  • Raised beds recommended in flood-prone zones.

🧪 Soil Type and pH

  • Grows well in sandy loam, coastal alluvial, and red soils.
  • pH range: 5.5 to 7.0.
  • Avoid waterlogged or saline soils; use lime to correct acidity if needed.

🌤 Climate Requirements

  • Requires warm, humid climate with 70–80% relative humidity.
  • Rainfall: 1200–2500 mm/year.
  • Tolerates moderate drought but not frost or extreme cold.

🌰 Seed Variety and Treatment

  • Select bright yellow-husked nuts from disease-free palms.
  • Treat with fungicide (Carbendazim) and insecticide (Chlorpyrifos) before sowing.
  • Nursery period: 10–12 months; transplant when 6–8 leaves appear.

📏 Sowing Method and Spacing

  • Spacing: 6.5 m × 6.5 m or 7 m × 7 m for better sunlight penetration.
  • Use square planting for uniform growth.
  • Plant during monsoon or irrigated season.

💊 Fertilizer Schedule

  • Year 1: 40 g N, 30 g P, 100 g K/month.
  • Mature palms: 500 g N, 320 g P, 1200 g K/year.
  • Foliar sprays of micronutrients every 6 months.

💧 Irrigation Management

  • Drip system: 40–60 liters/day/palm.
  • Mulch with coconut husk, dry leaves, or coir waste.
  • Monitor soil moisture using IoT sensors for precision irrigation.

🐛 Weed and Pest Control

  • Regular weeding or use of leguminous cover crops.
  • Pests: Mealybugs, mites—control with neem oil and soap sprays.
  • Diseases: Bud rot—prevent with Bordeaux mixture and proper drainage.

⏳ Harvesting Time and Yield

  • Begins bearing in 3–4 years.
  • Yield: ~70–80 nuts/palm/year.
  • Harvest every 30–40 days for tender coconut water.

📦 Post-Harvest Handling

  • Grade nuts based on size and husk color.
  • Store in shaded, ventilated areas.
  • Ideal for retail and export markets due to attractive appearance.
