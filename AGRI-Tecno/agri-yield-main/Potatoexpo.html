🥔 Kufri Giriraj Potato Cultivation Guide

1. Land and Soil Preparation

Plough the field 3–4 times to make a fine tilth.

Form ridges and furrows at 60 cm spacing.

Incorporate FYM/compost (20–25 tons/ha) well into the soil.

Proper drainage is essential as standing water causes tuber rot.

2. Soil Type and pH

Grows well in sandy loam to loamy soils.

Soil pH: 5.0 – 6.5 (slightly acidic, common in Nilgiris).

Avoid saline and alkaline soils.

3. Climate Requirements

Kufri Giriraj is suitable for hilly and temperate regions.

Best temperature: 18°C – 22°C.

Requires cool nights for better tuber bulking.

Cannot tolerate extreme heat.

4. Seed Variety and Treatment

Use certified seed tubers of Kufri Giriraj.

Select tubers of 30–50 g size with healthy sprouts.

Treat with Mancozeb 0.25% solution for 30 minutes.

Dry in shade before planting.

5. Sowing Method and Spacing

Plant on ridges and furrows.

Spacing: 60 cm between ridges × 20 cm between plants.

Planting depth: 6–8 cm.

Ensure tubers are placed with sprouts facing upward.

6. Fertilizer Schedule (per hectare)

Basal Application:

  • FYM – 20–25 tons
  • Urea – 120 kg
  • SSP – 250 kg
  • MOP – 120 kg

Top Dressing: (30–35 days after planting): Apply remaining 120 kg urea.

Note: Kufri Giriraj responds well to balanced NPK, excessive nitrogen delays maturity.

7. Irrigation Management

Give first irrigation immediately after planting.

Repeat at 7–10 day intervals depending on soil moisture.

Stop irrigation 10–12 days before harvest.

Avoid waterlogging (causes late blight and tuber cracking).

8. Weed and Pest Control

Weeds: Do 2 hand weedings (20 and 40 days). Earthing up is essential.

Pests:

  • Potato tuber moth – cover exposed tubers with soil, use pheromone traps.
  • Aphids & whiteflies – spray Dimethoate 0.05%.

Diseases:

  • Kufri Giriraj is tolerant to late blight, but preventive spray of Mancozeb 0.25% is advised.
  • For early blight, spray Chlorothalonil 0.2%.

9. Harvesting Time and Yield

Crop duration: 110–120 days.

Harvest when vines dry and skins of tubers harden.

Average yield: 30–35 tons/ha under good management.

10. Post-Harvest Handling

Cure tubers under shade for 7–10 days for skin hardening.

Grade potatoes into seed size and table purpose.

Store in cold storage at 4°C and 85–90% RH.

Transport in jute/gunny bags with proper ventilation.
