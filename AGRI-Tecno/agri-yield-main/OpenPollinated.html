🌱 Hill Garlic (Ooty Garlic) Cultivation Guide

1. Land and Soil Preparation

  • Land should be well-ploughed 3–4 times until fine tilth is achieved.
  • Raised beds or ridges and furrows are preferred to avoid water stagnation.
  • Application of well-decomposed FYM (20–25 t/ha) during land preparation enriches the soil.

2. Soil Type and pH

  • Grows best in loamy to sandy loam soils rich in organic matter.
  • Optimum soil pH: 6.0–7.5 (slightly acidic to neutral).

3. Climate Requirements

  • Hill garlic is grown in cool and subtropical climates (like Nilgiris).
  • Requires 12–20°C temperature for good bulb development.
  • Sensitive to waterlogging but tolerates mild drought.

4. Seed Variety and Treatment

  • Variety: Ooty 1 (Hill Garlic / Malai Poondu).
  • Use healthy cloves (4–6 g each) for planting.
  • Before sowing, cloves should be treated with Trichoderma viride (4 g/kg) or fungicide solution to prevent soil-borne diseases.

5. Sowing Method and Spacing

  • Time of sowing: September–October (ideal for hill regions).
  • Cloves are planted manually on ridges, 3–4 cm deep.
  • Spacing: 15 cm between rows × 10 cm between plants.
  • Seed rate: 500–600 kg cloves per hectare.

6. Fertilizer Schedule

  • Basal: FYM 20–25 t/ha + 50 kg P₂O₅/ha.
  • NPK Recommendation: 100:50:50 kg/ha.
  • Apply 50% Nitrogen at planting.
  • Remaining Nitrogen in 2–3 split doses during growth.
  • Foliar spray of micronutrients (Zn & B) improves bulb size.

7. Irrigation Management

  • First irrigation immediately after planting.
  • Subsequent irrigations every 8–10 days depending on soil moisture.
  • Critical stages: bulb initiation & bulb development.
  • Avoid waterlogging (causes bulb rot).

8. Weed and Pest Control

  • Weeding: 2–3 hand weedings or use of pre-emergence herbicide like Pendimethalin.
  • Pests: Thrips & aphids – controlled by neem oil or systemic insecticides.
  • Diseases: Purple blotch, stemphylium blight – controlled by spraying Mancozeb or Copper oxychloride.

9. Harvesting Time and Yield

  • Crop duration: 120–150 days after sowing.
  • Harvest when leaves start yellowing and drying.
  • Expected yield: 6–8 t/ha in hills.

10. Post-Harvest Handling

  • Bulbs are carefully lifted, cleaned, and dried under shade for 10–15 days.
  • Tops are trimmed, and bulbs are tied into bunches for storage.
  • Stored in well-ventilated rooms (garlic keeps longer in hill regions due to its tight cloves and low moisture).
