🌱 Alphonso Mango Cultivation Guide

1. Land and Soil Preparation

▼
  • Deep ploughing (2–3 times) to make the soil friable.
  • Remove weeds, stones, and old stumps.
  • Dig pits of 1 × 1 × 1 m at proper spacing.
  • Fill pits with topsoil + 25–30 kg FYM + 2–3 kg neem cake + 1 kg single super phosphate before planting.

2. Soil Type and pH

▼
  • Best suited: well-drained lateritic, alluvial, or sandy loam soils.
  • Ideal pH: 6.0–7.5.
  • Avoid heavy clay, waterlogged, or saline-alkaline soils.

3. Climate Requirements

▼
  • Grows best in tropical and subtropical climates.
  • Ideal temperature: 20–35°C.
  • Annual rainfall: 750–2500 mm (well distributed).
  • A dry spell before flowering improves fruit set and quality.

4. Seed Variety and Treatment

▼
  • Variety: Alphonso Mango (Hapus/Hasus).
  • Plant grafted seedlings from reliable nurseries (avoid seedling plants).
  • Treat grafts with fungicide (Carbendazim @ 1 g/L) before planting to reduce root rot.

5. Planting Method and Spacing

▼
  • Planting season: June–July (rainfed) or December–January (irrigated conditions).
  • Spacing: 9 × 9 m (≈120 plants/ha).
  • Plant graft at the same depth as in nursery.
  • Provide staking to protect young grafts from wind.

6. Fertilizer Schedule

▼
  • FYM: 25–30 kg/tree/year.
  • NPK Dose (per tree, after 3rd year):
  • 1st year: 200 g N, 100 g P, 200 g K.
  • Increase gradually up to 1 kg N, 500 g P, 1 kg K for adult trees.
  • Apply fertilizers in two splits: before flowering & after fruit set.
  • Foliar spray: ZnSO₄ (0.5%) + Borax (0.2%) to improve fruit quality.

7. Irrigation Management

▼
  • Young plants: irrigate once in 7–10 days during dry season.
  • Bearing trees: irrigate at critical stages – flowering, fruit set, and fruit development.
  • Stop irrigation 10–15 days before flowering to encourage good bloom.
  • Drip irrigation recommended for efficient water use.

8. Weed and Pest Control

▼
  • Use mulching (dry leaves, grass, black polythene) to suppress weeds.
  • Major pests: Mango hopper, fruit fly, mealy bug.
  • Control with neem oil (5%) or approved insecticides.
  • Major diseases: Powdery mildew, anthracnose, sooty mold.
  • Prevent with Mancozeb (0.25%) or Carbendazim (0.1%) sprays.

9. Harvesting and Yield

▼
  • Bearing starts: 4–5 years after planting (grafts).
  • Maturity: 90–120 days after fruit set.
  • Average yield: 8–10 t/ha in initial years; 12–15 t/ha at full bearing.
  • Fruits are medium-sized (150–300 g), golden yellow with saffron-colored pulp, fibreless, and very aromatic.

10. Post-harvest Handling

▼
  • Harvest fruits at mature green or light yellow stage (not fully ripe).
  • Grade fruits by size, shape, and color.
  • Pack in ventilated wooden or plastic crates.
  • Shelf life: 7–10 days at room temp; 2–3 weeks in cold storage (12–13°C, 85–90% RH).
  • Uses: Premium table fruit, export, pulp, puree, ice creams, and confectionery.
