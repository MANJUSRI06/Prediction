🧬 Chandrasankara Hybrid (COD × WCT) Cultivation Guide
Jump to:

🌱 Land and Soil Preparation

▼
  • Deep ploughing followed by pit digging (1m³).
  • Mix topsoil with 10 kg FYM, 1 kg neem cake, 1 kg superphosphate.
  • Ensure proper drainage and raised beds in clayey soils.

🧪 Soil Type and pH

▼
  • Suitable for loamy, laterite, and red soils.
  • pH range: 5.5 to 7.5.
  • Soil testing recommended before planting; amend with lime or gypsum as needed.

🌤 Climate Requirements

▼
  • Requires warm, humid climate with 25–32°C temperature.
  • Rainfall: 1000–3000 mm/year.
  • Performs well in both coastal and inland zones with irrigation.

🌰 Seed Variety and Treatment

▼
  • Hybrid seed nuts from certified sources (TNAU or CPCRI).
  • Treat with fungicide and insecticide before sowing.
  • Nursery period: 10–12 months; transplant when seedlings are robust.

📏 Sowing Method and Spacing

▼
  • Spacing: 7.5 m × 7.5 m (due to larger canopy).
  • Triangular planting preferred for better sunlight utilization.
  • Plant during monsoon or with assured irrigation.

💊 Fertilizer Schedule

▼
  • Year 1: 60 g N, 40 g P, 150 g K/month.
  • Mature palms: 600 g N, 400 g P, 1500 g K/year.
  • Apply in 3 split doses; supplement with micronutrients and biofertilizers.

💧 Irrigation Management

▼
  • Drip irrigation: 60–80 liters/day/palm.
  • Mulching and basin irrigation during dry spells.
  • Use IoT-based moisture sensors for efficient water use.

🐛 Weed and Pest Control

▼
  • Manual weeding or use of cover crops like sunhemp.
  • Pests: Red palm weevil, scale insects—control with pheromone traps and neem sprays.
  • Diseases: Leaf spot, bud rot—prevent with copper fungicides and sanitation.

⏳ Harvesting Time and Yield

▼
  • Starts bearing in 4–5 years.
  • Yield: ~100–120 nuts/palm/year.
  • Suitable for both tender coconut and copra/oil extraction.

📦 Post-Harvest Handling

▼
  • Separate tender and mature nuts.
  • Dry mature nuts for copra; store in ventilated sheds.
  • Ideal for processing units and commercial plantations.
